# Required Deliverables

Every team repository must contain evidence for all deliverables below.

## D1 — Discovery & Data Quality Assessment
**Due:** End of Week 1  
**Location:** `docs/01-discovery/`

Required:
- source-file inventory;
- file sizes and row counts;
- customer and timestamp grain;
- time coverage;
- duplicate/missing checks;
- tariff-field assessment;
- data-quality risks;
- initial processing strategy.

## D2 — Scalable Ingestion & Transformation Pipeline
**Due:** Initial version by end of Week 3  
**Location:** `src/`, `sql/`, `docs/02-data-model/`

Required:
- reproducible ingestion;
- efficient handling of large raw files;
- documented transformations;
- reusable analytical tables;
- time-based aggregations;
- customer-level structures;
- execution instructions.

## D3 — KPI & Metric Catalogue
**Due:** End of Week 3  
**Location:** `docs/03-kpi-catalogue/`

Include:
- metric name;
- purpose;
- definition;
- calculation;
- grain;
- source fields;
- exclusions;
- limitations.

Expected metric areas:
- consumption;
- peak demand;
- load factor/load profile;
- tariff exposure;
- tariff response;
- customer segment;
- seasonal usage;
- demand concentration.

## D4 — Demand Pattern Analysis
**Due:** End of Week 4  
**Location:** `analysis/` or `notebooks/`

Required:
- time-of-day patterns;
- weekday/weekend differences;
- seasonal patterns;
- customer-level variability;
- peak-demand analysis.

## D5 — Tariff & Demand-Response Assessment
**Due:** End of Week 5  
**Location:** `docs/04-tariff-analysis/` and analytical code

Required:
- comparison methodology;
- tariff-group analysis;
- high/normal/low price-period analysis where supported;
- effect-size or response measures;
- limitations and confounding considerations;
- responsible interpretation.

## D6 — Customer Segmentation / Flexibility Opportunity Analysis
**Due:** End of Week 6  
**Location:** `analysis/` and `docs/05-customer-segmentation/`

Required:
- justified segmentation method;
- segment definitions;
- behavioural characteristics;
- operational/commercial interpretation;
- limitations.

## D7 — Management Dashboard / Decision-Support Product
**Due:** Draft Week 6; final Week 7  
**Location:** `dashboard/`

Should support:
- demand monitoring;
- peak-period analysis;
- tariff comparisons;
- customer segment exploration;
- clear management interpretation.

## D8 — Technical Handover
**Due:** Week 8  
**Location:** `docs/06-technical-handover/`

Must explain:
- repository structure;
- data acquisition;
- environment/setup;
- ingestion design;
- transformation logic;
- data model;
- metric definitions;
- how to reproduce outputs;
- known limitations;
- performance/scalability considerations.

## D9 — Executive Briefing
**Due:** Week 8  
**Location:** `deliverables/executive-briefing/`

Maximum two pages or equivalent concise briefing.

## D10 — Final Presentation
**Due:** Mettelo Demo Day  
**Location:** `deliverables/presentation/`

15–20 minute presentation plus Q&A.

## D11 — Contribution Record
**Due:** Final submission  
**Location:** `CONTRIBUTIONS.md`

For each member:
- role;
- responsibilities;
- main deliverables;
- linked evidence;
- presentation contribution.
