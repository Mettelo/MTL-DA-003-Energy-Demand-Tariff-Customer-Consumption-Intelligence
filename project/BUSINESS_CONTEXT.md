# Business Context

## Client Profile

**Client:** Confidential Regional Energy Network & Retail Partner  
**Sector:** Energy / Utilities  
**Operating Context:** Household electricity demand, network planning and retail tariff strategy

## Why This Engagement Was Commissioned

Leadership is under pressure to understand changing electricity-demand patterns and the role that flexible household consumption may play in future network planning.

Current reporting has several weaknesses:

1. demand is often viewed at aggregate daily or monthly level;
2. peak-period analysis is inconsistent;
3. retail tariff analysis is separated from network-demand analysis;
4. customer-level load patterns are not routinely segmented;
5. teams lack one reusable analytical layer for half-hourly smart-meter data;
6. the organisation has limited evidence on how dynamic price signals influence household behaviour.

## Stakeholders

### Director of Customer Energy & Flexibility — Project Sponsor
Needs evidence on customer behaviour and demand-shifting opportunities.

### Head of Network Planning
Needs visibility of peak periods, demand concentration and load-shape variability.

### Head of Retail Pricing
Needs evidence on the relationship between tariff signals and consumption behaviour.

### Customer Insights Lead
Needs meaningful customer segments and interpretable behavioural patterns.

### Operations Planning Lead
Needs practical demand indicators that can support operational planning.

### Data & Analytics Lead
Needs a scalable, reproducible analytical approach that can be handed over.

## Decision Context

The executive team is considering future investment in:

- time-of-use tariffs;
- demand-response programmes;
- customer flexibility propositions;
- improved forecasting;
- targeted customer communications;
- more scalable smart-meter analytics.

The project should help determine where the available evidence is strong enough to support further investment and where further experimentation is required.
